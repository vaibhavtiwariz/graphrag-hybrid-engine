# Zephyr X9 Field Notes

The Zephyr X9 is a prototype delivery drone piloted with the city of Boulder.
Its flight controller is the StormCore FC7 built by Redwood Avionics.
Battery packs use six VoltMax 90 cells from Aurora Power Systems.
Incident TRIAL-88 (June 2026) documented GPS drift during high winds; the fix was
firmware 0.9.4 authored by the Flight Systems team under lead Omar Haddad.
Escalations for Zephyr trials route to program manager Sofia Lindqvist.
