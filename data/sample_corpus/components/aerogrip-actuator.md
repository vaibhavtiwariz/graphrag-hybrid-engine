# AeroGrip Actuator

The AeroGrip actuator is a harmonic-drive actuator with a brushless DC motor used in the
AtlasArm industrial arm.

## Supply chain

- Housings: CNC-machined by Kestrel Metals in Tucson, AZ.
- Motor windings: wound in-house at the Fremont plant.

## Quality history

Batch A-2231 (August 2024) housings from Kestrel Metals machining line 4 received improper
thread-lock application, causing overheating under sustained load. The issue was tracked as
incident INC-1042 and remediated by replacing affected housings and a supplier corrective action.
