# VoltCore 4000 Battery Cell

The VoltCore 4000 is a 21700-format lithium-ion cell rated at 4000 mAh, manufactured exclusively
by Helix Cell Works in Osaka, Japan.

## Specifications

- Cycle life: 800 cycles to 80% capacity.
- Operating temperature: -20 to 60 degrees C.
- Shipping class: UN3481 (lithium-ion contained in equipment).

## Usage

Four VoltCore 4000 cells form the NimbusOne drone battery pack. Battery warranty claims follow
policy WP-9 with 12 months of coverage. Failed cells are returned via supplier RMA handled by
Supply Chain analyst Dana Kim; Helix Cell Works honors an RMA turnaround SLA of 21 days.
