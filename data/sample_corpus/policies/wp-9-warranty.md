# Policy WP-9: Warranty Terms

Standard warranty coverage for Nimbus Robotics products:

- Products (NimbusOne, AtlasArm, HaloDock): 24 months from purchase.
- Batteries and cells, including VoltCore 4000: 12 months.
- Actuators, including AeroGrip: 18 months.

## Claims process

1. Field engineer logs a warranty case with a diagnostic bundle attached.
2. Supply Chain analyst Dana Kim issues the supplier RMA:
   - Helix Cell Works RMA turnaround SLA: 21 days.
   - Kestrel Metals RMA turnaround SLA: 14 days.
3. Out-of-warranty units qualify for the replacement program at a 15% discount.

Batteries ship under UN3481 Class 9 labeling requirements.
