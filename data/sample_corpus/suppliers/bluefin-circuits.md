# BlueFin Circuits

BlueFin Circuits is an electronics manufacturer supplying printed circuit boards and
electromechanical parts to Nimbus Robotics.

## Supplied parts

- SkyLink X2 radio module (complete module build) for NimbusOne and HaloDock.
- AtlasArm controller PCB.
- HaloDock power relay, 20 A, revision A.

## Quality history

The revision-A 20 A relay is undersized for full 900 W simultaneous dock loads; breakers tripped
at three depots in February 2025 under incident INC-1090. A 30 A revision-B relay is scheduled as
the permanent fix. The SkyLink X2 firmware 2.3.1 watchdog defect (incident INC-1077) was also on
BlueFin-built hardware and fixed in signed firmware 2.3.2.
