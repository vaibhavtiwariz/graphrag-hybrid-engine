# Kestrel Metals

Kestrel Metals is a CNC machining supplier based in Tucson, AZ. It machines AeroGrip actuator
housings for the AtlasArm and fabricates HaloDock enclosures.

## Supplier facts

- Quality contact: Rosa Delgado.
- On-time delivery rate: 96%.
- Machining lines: line 2 and line 4 (Tucson plant).

## Quality history

Housings from batch A-2231, produced on machining line 4 in August 2024, had improper
thread-lock application. This caused AtlasArm actuator overheating tracked under incident
INC-1042 and resulted in a formal supplier corrective action (CAPA) with quarterly audits by the
Nimbus quality team led by Priya Nair.
