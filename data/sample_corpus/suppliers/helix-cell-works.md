# Helix Cell Works

Helix Cell Works is a battery cell manufacturer based in Osaka, Japan. It is the sole supplier
of the VoltCore 4000 cell used in NimbusOne battery packs.

## Supplier facts

- Quality contact: Kenji Watanabe.
- RMA turnaround SLA: 21 days.
- Certification: ISO 9001:2015.

## Escalation path

Battery quality issues route through Priya Nair (Quality Lead) to the supplier's quality office.
Logistics for failed-cell returns are coordinated by Dana Kim (Supply Chain Analyst).
