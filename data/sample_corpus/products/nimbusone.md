# NimbusOne Delivery Drone

NimbusOne is Nimbus Robotics' flagship autonomous delivery drone, released in March 2024.
It carries a 5 kg payload with a 32 km operating range. The fleet of 1,200 units serves 14 cities.

## Bill of materials highlights

- Telemetry radio: SkyLink X2 module (firmware line 2.x) built by BlueFin Circuits.
- Battery pack: four VoltCore 4000 lithium-ion cells manufactured by Helix Cell Works.
- Final assembly plant: Fremont, CA.

## Service notes

Mid-flight telemetry dropouts observed across the fleet in May 2024 were tracked under incident
INC-1077 and resolved by a signed firmware upgrade. Battery warranty coverage follows policy WP-9.
