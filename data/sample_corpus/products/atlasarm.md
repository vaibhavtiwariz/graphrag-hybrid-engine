# AtlasArm Industrial Robotic Arm

AtlasArm is a six-axis industrial robotic arm released in September 2023 with a 12 kg payload.
The installed base is 380 units across 45 customer plants.

## Bill of materials highlights

- Actuators: AeroGrip actuator assemblies; housings are CNC-machined by Kestrel Metals.
- Controller PCB: supplied by BlueFin Circuits.

## Service notes

Sustained-load overheating on two plants in September 2024 was tracked under incident INC-1042
and traced to a machining defect on AeroGrip housings. Actuator warranty coverage follows policy
WP-9 (18 months for actuators).
