# Incident INC-1077: NimbusOne Fleet Telemetry Dropouts

- Reported: May 2024
- Product: NimbusOne delivery drone (1,200 unit fleet, 14 cities)
- Severity: major, no crashes

## Root cause

SkyLink X2 firmware 2.3.1 contained a watchdog reset defect that dropped the telemetry link under
sustained airframe vibration. Average outage per flight was 17 minutes.

## Remediation

- Fleet-wide upgrade to signed firmware 2.3.2 completed June 2024.
- The signed image satisfies security standard SEC-14; compliance audited quarterly by Marcus
  Webb's firmware team.
- Incident owned by Marcus Webb (Firmware Lead).
