# Incident INC-1090: HaloDock Breaker Trips

- Reported: February 2025
- Product: HaloDock charging dock (900 W)
- Sites affected: three depots
- Severity: moderate

## Root cause

The revision-A 20 A power relay supplied by BlueFin Circuits is undersized for full 900 W
simultaneous charge sessions, tripping depot breakers.

## Remediation

- Interim: dock firmware 3.1 scheduler staggers simultaneous sessions.
- Permanent: 30 A revision-B relay from BlueFin Circuits, depot retrofit in progress.
- Field rollout coordinated by Lena Ortiz (Field Service Manager) with the Hardware Platform
  team.
