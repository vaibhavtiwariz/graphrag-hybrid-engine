# Incident INC-1042: AtlasArm Actuator Overheating

- Reported: September 2024
- Product: AtlasArm industrial arm
- Sites affected: two customer plants (45-unit installed base)
- Severity: major

## Root cause

AeroGrip actuator housings from batch A-2231, machined on Kestrel Metals line 4 in August 2024,
received improper thread-lock application. Sustained load caused the housings to overheat.

## Remediation

- Replace all batch A-2231 housings in the field (completed November 2024).
- Supplier corrective action (CAPA) opened with Kestrel Metals; quality contact Rosa Delgado.
- Quarterly audits of Kestrel machining lines by Priya Nair's quality team.

Warranty treatment followed policy WP-9: actuators carry 18 months of coverage.
